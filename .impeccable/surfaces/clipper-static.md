## Direction contract

THESIS: Every AI judgment Clipper makes renders as a live instrument reading beside the footage, never a bare verdict — the app is a colorist's grading bay, refusing the glass-card AI-dashboard arrangement this category defaults to.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral mid-dark "reference gray" surround (~#1c1e1e, genuinely neutral — no blue/purple AI-dashboard tint, the way a grading bay stays neutral so color judgment isn't biased). Signal colors are diegetic, not decorative: scope-green for pass/level, amber for caution, true vectorscope red/cyan for fail/over-range — the same three colors do double duty as the score-verdict palette. Tabular mono (IBM Plex Mono) for every number — timecodes, scores, durations; a plain grotesk for labels. Every panel is a bordered "scope module": hairline frame, small tracked-caps label top-left, a tick-marked graticule ground, the live reading plotted as a real meter/scope graphic, never a number alone in a rounded card. Controls are deck/transport hardware — rectangular hairline buttons with corner ticks, fader-style sliders with a tick scale — never pill buttons or soft shadow cards. Raised from the darkroom exposure-record challenger: the clip editor's layer stack (captions/zoom/B-roll/overlays) renders as overlapping translucent sheets you slide and toggle, each carrying its own small meter reading — not a plain settings list.

STORY: The user feels like they've walked into a grading bay. Picking clips, reading a score, tuning captions or audio — every action reads as adjusting and reading a real instrument, never clicking a dashboard card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: New clips page. Left column: the video/brief intake as a patch-bay input module (drop zone framed in tick-marked graticule structure, not a skeuomorphic film-reel icon). Right column: Look + settings as a rack of deck modules — toggle switches and tick-scaled faders, flush hairline-divided panels, no floating shadowed cards. The clip-quality score, wherever shown, is a small meter bridge: one bar per score part, lit against a green/amber/red zone scale, its measured evidence captioned directly beneath like a meter's calibration label.

FORM: Telecine color-grading suite (own-ranked direction, index 1 of 7; seed key 8e8823fd). Assigned index 6 (Descript-style transcript-timeline) was raised past on distinctiveness. Catalog challenger "operate-b-exposure-record-zone-sheets" won on product clarity for layered-edit structure; its translucent-overlay-sheet discipline is donated into the clip editor's layer stack, named above.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
